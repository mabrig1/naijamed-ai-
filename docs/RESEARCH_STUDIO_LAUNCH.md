## Client workflow

1. Researcher creates an account.
2. Researcher selects a defined service and supplies project information.
3. Mabrig HealthOS creates a MongoDB order record.
4. Paystack checkout is initialized.
5. On return, the app verifies reference, amount, currency and customer email with Paystack.
6. The order becomes `intake` only after secure payment verification.
7. Methods, parameters, data provenance and deliverables should be stored with the project so the work is reproducible.

## Research authority strategy

### Polyherbal Synergy Index (PSI-β)

PSI-β is implemented as an experimental project framework, not a therapeutic efficacy score. Its research sequence is:

1. standardize plant and compound identities;
2. screen pharmacokinetic and toxicity flags;
3. map compounds to targets and disease pathways;
4. run reproducible docking/binding analyses;
5. construct polyherbal interaction networks;
6. quantify target coverage, complementarity and redundancy;
7. pre-register wet-lab validation criteria before efficacy claims.

The scientific objective is to publish a transparent methodology that can be independently tested. A future formal index should only be presented after mathematical definition, benchmarking, sensitivity analysis and experimental validation.

### West African ethnobotanical bioinformatics niche

Prioritize research questions with strong regional relevance, clearly documented plant identity, reproducible compound sources, and ethically appropriate claims. Build reusable datasets and computational workflows around locally important disease areas rather than repeatedly publishing isolated docking papers.

### In-silico-to-product pipeline

Computational evidence -> experimental validation -> standardized extract/formulation -> IP review -> safety/quality evidence -> regulatory pathway -> commercialization.

Do not market computational predictions as proof of treatment, safety, patentability, or regulatory acceptance.

## Academic integrity rules

The Studio may perform analysis, visualization, methods consulting, training and proposal-development support. It must not fabricate results, invent citations, falsify experiments, conceal authorship obligations, or guarantee publication/defense outcomes. Researchers remain responsible for their thesis/article conclusions, ethics approvals, source data and final submitted work.

# MediNaija — 3-minute demo video script

## 0:00–0:25 — Start with phone
Show the MediNaija landing page on a mobile viewport.

Voice:
“MediNaija is a chronic-care companion built for Nigerians managing diabetes, high blood pressure and asthma. You start with your phone number, not a long registration form.”

Open the consent screen, accept health-data processing and optional SMS reminders, then request the OTP.

## 0:25–0:55 — Onboarding
Enter the OTP and show the three onboarding screens.

Voice:
“Tell MediNaija what condition you manage, where you currently get your medicines and the monthly budget you are already working with. The questions are simple and designed for low-bandwidth phones.”

Choose hypertension, community pharmacy and ₦10,000.

## 0:55–1:25 — Dashboard and adherence
Open the dashboard.

Voice:
“The dashboard keeps the important things visible: your next refill date, adherence streak and monthly spend. A daily check-in takes one tap.”

Tap “Yes, I did” for the medicine check-in. Briefly switch the network offline and reload cached data.

Voice:
“The last 30 days can stay available offline, so poor network does not erase the care routine.”

## 1:25–2:05 — Subscribe and pay
Open Plans and choose Hypertension Plus.

Voice:
“Plans are priced in Naira. The browser never decides the amount — MediNaija loads the authoritative price from the database before starting payment.”

Tap Subscribe to open Paystack Inline. Then demonstrate the “Pay with USSD” option.

Voice:
“For customers who prefer USSD, Paystack can restrict checkout to its USSD channel. The wider refill and status USSD menu will use the exact short code assigned by our Nigerian aggregator — we do not invent one.”

## 2:05–2:30 — Pharmacy and refill
Open the partner pharmacy locator and use location.

Voice:
“MediNaija searches partner pharmacies within five kilometres. Demo records are clearly marked until each live partner is PCN-verified.”

Show the pickup/delivery concept and explain that delivery dispatch will connect to GIG Logistics or Kwik after commercial/API onboarding.

## 2:30–2:50 — SMS refill reminder
Show the refill reminder flow.

Voice:
“Three days before a refill, users who opted into SMS receive a short Termii reminder. If they have not opened the app, care does not depend on push notifications.”

## 2:50–3:00 — Close
Return to the dashboard.

Voice:
“MediNaija brings medicines, refill support and everyday adherence into one practical Nigerian chronic-care plan. Simple enough for 3G. Useful even when the smartphone is not.”

Methods, source and limits

This practical fits a new ridge regression with a fixed penalty of 1. Inputs are molecular weight, hydrogen-bond donor count, ring count, rotatable-bond count and polar surface area. The benchmark excludes the source file’s existing ESOL prediction from the inputs.

Five preassigned folds keep identical rounded descriptor vectors together. Each fit uses only its training fold complement to standardise inputs, estimate coefficients and compute the mean-only baseline. Chemical relatives may occur in different folds. The results measure performance within this dataset. Further testing on independent chemical series and measurements under defined conditions is needed before using it to guide a project.

Water solubility is one developability property. This analysis does not establish a compound’s target activity, therapeutic effect or safety.
